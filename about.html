About This Game

Politician or Criminal is a social experiment–style quiz that challenges how quickly we judge people based on appearance alone.

What’s the Idea?

You are shown an image of a real person and must decide: Politician or Criminal.

After your choice, the truth is revealed — including the person’s name and either their political role or their crime.

Why This Game Exists

Humans judge fast. Sometimes unfairly. This game exposes how unreliable first impressions can be — and how easily authority, danger, or innocence can be misread.

Important Disclaimer

All individuals shown are real public figures. This game is for educational and awareness purposes only. It does not promote violence, hate, or harm.

How Many Questions?

Currently, the game contains 40 unique questions. The order is randomized on every refresh to keep the experience fresh.
